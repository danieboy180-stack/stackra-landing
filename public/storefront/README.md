# Storefront tile photos

Photos for the small floating boxes around the checkout card in the
"Turn customer interest into orders." section.

The list at the top of components/browse-to-order.tsx decides which files are
used. They are shared out across the boxes, and each box fades through its own
photos. To add a photo: upload it to this folder, then add its filename to that
list. The big card and the pill under it stay empty.
